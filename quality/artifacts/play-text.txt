Play Primordial Sea Online

Primordial Sea is free to play in a browser on mobile and desktop. Use the Official game button to open the developer’s release page.

Official game
How the controls work
1
Aim around the disk

Drag to choose a release point anywhere around the gravity disk.

2
Release the body

Let go to drop the current body into the field before its fuse burns out.

3
Match identical bodies

When two bodies of the same stage meet, they merge into the next stage.

4
Respond to pressure

Higher scores shorten the fuse. Enemy mode also adds dark stars that must be tapped down.

If browser play does not load

Open the developer’s itch.io page directly for its current browser build. This guide does not pin a potentially outdated upload or embed a third-party game.

Progress and availability are controlled by the official game, not this guide. This site does not mirror or redistribute the game files.
