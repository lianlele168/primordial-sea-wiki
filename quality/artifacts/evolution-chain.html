Primordial Sea Evolution Chain

The normal disk has ten named stages. Every step requires two matching bodies, so a Water Planet represents 512 Cosmic Dust bodies in the theoretical no-loss case.

How the merge math works

Two identical bodies create one body at the next stage. Moving up n stages therefore requires 2 to the power of n source bodies. For example, one Rain World theoretically requires eight Molten Cores.

This is a planning minimum, not a score forecast. Physics, fuse explosions, enemy attacks, and imperfect drops can increase what a real run needs.

What comes after Water Planet

The official page confirms that hidden disks can evolve worlds beyond the Water Planet, including golden and rainbow bodies. The description does not list post-Water-Planet names or recipes.

Frequently asked questions

How many stages are in the normal Primordial Sea chain?

There are ten named stages from Cosmic Dust through Water Planet.

What comes after Ocean World?

Two Ocean Worlds merge into a Water Planet in the normal chain.

Can worlds evolve beyond Water Planet?

Yes, but the release description only establishes further hidden-disk evolution; it does not list that chain.
