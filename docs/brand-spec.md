# Primordial Sea Guide - Visual Source Spec

This is an unofficial guide. The visual system is derived from the official itch.io cover and gameplay screenshots, not a fabricated brand identity.

- `public/primordial-sea-cover.png`: official itch.io cover / OG image

Source page: https://float-u-space.itch.io/primordial-sea

## Palette

- Cosmic black: `#050713`
- Ink panel: `#101525`
- Tide cyan: `#36c7c2`
- Water highlight: `#9af0e9`
- Solar gold: `#efbd4e`
- Fuse coral: `#f45f70`
- Ice white: `#eef3ff`

## Typography and composition

- Editorial serif for large cosmic headings; compact geometric sans for controls and body copy.
- No generic purple SaaS gradient, fake live counters, decorative metrics, or stock imagery.
- Use actual gameplay screenshots where the user needs to understand the board.
- Cards stay at 8px radius or below. Sections remain unframed full-width bands.
- The evolution chain is the recurring visual signature.
